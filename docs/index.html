SpendQuill: Expense Tracker Privacy Policy

Published by thoughtfulbyte. Last updated November 21, 2026.

SpendQuill helps you record and organize your finances. Your ledger is stored on your device. You can use the app with an app PIN. Google sign-in, Google Drive backup, and SMS access are optional.

Information SpendQuill uses

  • Ledger information. The app stores transactions, amounts, dates, descriptions, notes, budgets, bills, account details and other information you enter or import. Account details can include bank names and account or card identifiers. The app uses these records to organize your ledger, calculate totals, and match transactions and bills to accounts. It does not connect to your bank to obtain credentials or initiate payments.
  • Files and photos you choose. SpendQuill reads import and restore files you select, and stores copies of receipt images and profile photos you choose to add. Optional profile information such as a display name and phone number is stored on your device.
  • App security. SpendQuill stores a salted verification value for your app PIN. If you enable biometric unlock, Android performs the biometric check and returns the result. SpendQuill does not receive or store your fingerprint or face data.

Optional SMS access

If you enable automatic SMS detection, the app reads newly received SMS to identify financial transactions and bill notices. If you start an SMS history scan and grant permission, the app reads received SMS in the date range you choose, which can include read and unread messages. You can also paste a message without granting inbox access.

SpendQuill processes message bodies, senders and timestamps on the device to identify amounts, dates, merchants and account references. It is designed to skip one-time passwords and unrelated messages. Recognized financial messages can be saved with their original text and associated details, including messages saved for account review. Saved financial SMS can be included in a ledger export or backup you choose to create. SpendQuill does not upload your SMS inbox or use messages for advertising.

You can disable automatic detection or revoke SMS permissions in Android settings. This stops future access. To remove information already saved in the ledger, use the deletion options below.

Google sign-in, backups and sharing

  • Google sign-in. If you choose to sign in with Google, Google handles authentication. SpendQuill stores your verified Google email address, account identifier and available display name on your device to recognize the ledger owner. Thoughtfulbyte does not maintain a sign-in server or receive your Google password.
  • Google Drive backup. If you separately authorize Drive backup, SpendQuill uploads and restores backup copies in its private app data folder in your Google Drive. The app uses this access to create, list, restore and prune its backups. Backups can include account and transaction details, saved financial SMS, bills, and receipt images. Your PIN and Google sign-in settings are not included. Daily backups run only if you enable them. Google processes the sign-in and backup data needed for these features under its Privacy Policy.
  • Local backups and exports. If you export or save a local backup, SpendQuill writes it to the location or storage provider you choose. That provider may sync or otherwise handle the file according to its terms and your settings.

Thoughtfulbyte does not sell your data, use it for advertising, or operate a server that receives your ledger or SMS inbox. SpendQuill’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Security

SpendQuill keeps its working ledger and receipt copies in Android’s private app storage. The app PIN and optional biometric unlock control access to the app. The database and exported files are not separately encrypted by SpendQuill. Connections used for Google services are made over HTTPS. Keep exported files and backups in a trusted location.

Retention and deletion

  • On the device. SpendQuill retains ledger records and saved messages until you remove them or erase the app’s storage. You can delete transactions, discard messages awaiting review and remove profile photos in the app. Moving an account to Deleted keeps its transaction history. To erase the local ledger, saved messages, profile, PIN settings and Google sign-in association together, open Android Settings → Apps → SpendQuill → Storage & cache → Clear storage, or uninstall SpendQuill. This does not delete your Google Account or copies stored elsewhere.
  • Backups. SpendQuill normally keeps the six newest managed copies at each configured backup location after successful backups. Disable daily backups to stop new scheduled copies. Delete exported or local backup files from the folder or storage provider where they were saved. To remove Drive backups, use Google Drive Settings → Manage apps and Delete hidden app data for SpendQuill, if available. You can also revoke SpendQuill’s Google access from your Google Account settings. Revoking access and deleting backup files are separate steps.
